What is a fraction?

A fraction represents a part of a whole. It has two main parts: the numerator and the denominator.

3
4
3/4

3 is the numerator.
4 is the denominator.

EXAMPLE

Understanding 3/4

Imagine a pizza divided into four equal pieces. If you have three pieces, you have three quarters of the pizza.

🍕
REMEMBER
  • The numerator tells us how many parts we have.
  • The denominator tells us how many equal parts make the whole.
  • Both numbers are important when reading a fraction.
